- **#866** The weekly hardware is three celebratory awards instead of five gongs. Bad Beat
  and Backdoor were one measure read from both ends and went as the weakest pair; Donkey went
  because it fired every week at whoever was already last, which in a room of six friends
  brands the same real person all season — the standings already say who is losing, and the
  app's job is to feed the trash talk, not throw the punch. In their place is **Bullseye**,
  for a publicly-declared All-In that landed: the most chat-worthy thing a week produces, and
  a decision the player made rather than a margin technicality. All three are re-voiced, the
  recap link now leads the card instead of trailing it, and the season Trophy shelf is gone —
  with Bullseye in the catalog its best line would have counted All-In hits with no rate
  behind them, the same flaw that retired the Big Game Hunter badge. Cosmetic throughout:
  nothing about the All-In reveal or scoring moves. Governed by ADR-0023 and ADR-0035;
  `weeklyAwards.ts`, `WeeklyHardware.svelte`, `AwardsGuide.svelte`, the `group_pick_cover`
  matview (new `weight` column), and the regenerated demo fixture.
